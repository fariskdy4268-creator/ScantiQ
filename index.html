SCANTIQ – Class Structural Scantling Suite

Six rule-based scantling calculators in one place. Choose the classification society and rule set; each calculator keeps its own projects, examples, Save / Open files and printed calculation report.

Notes
  • Switching between calculators keeps your work in each one; entries are also remembered in this browser.
  • Project files saved from one calculator open only in that calculator.
  • Scantlings are subject to approval by the classification society. These sheets are design aids.
