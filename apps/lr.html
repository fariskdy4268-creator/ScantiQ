4 · Basis & references

Basis of calculation

  • Two rule sets. Rules for Ships (January 2016): Pt 4 Ch 1 general cargo ship scantlings in the midship region – shell, decks, single and double bottom, framing, bulkheads, primary members, pillars – with the Pt 4 modifications for ferries, tugs, offshore supply / standby / anchor handling ships (minimum side shell, framing × 1,25 / 1,10, weather deck), barges and pontoons (Ch 5 framing tables) and fishing vessels (Ch 6 minimum shell); erections by Pt 3 Ch 8 and hull girder strength by Pt 3 Ch 4. Special Service Craft (July 2022): design loads of Pt 5 Ch 2 to Ch 5 and steel scantlings of Pt 6 Ch 3 (mono-hull), Pt 6 Ch 6 and Ch 7.
  • Ships – local reduction factors. FD = σD/σ and FB = σB/σ from the still water and wave moments with the offered ZD and ZB (σ = 175/kL), not less than 0,67 for plating and 0,75 for longitudinals, or 1,0 when not entered. Mw = f1 f2 0,1 C1 L² B (Cb + 0,7), Zmin = f1 kL C1 L² B (Cb + 0,7) 10⁻⁶. Cw = 0,0771 L e^(−0,0044 L); heads hT1, hT2, hT3 with Fλ; fatigue factor Fs; design heads of Pt 3 Ch 3 Table 3.5.1 with E.
  • SSC – loads. Displacement mode (Γ < 3 and Δ > 0,04 (LR B)^1,5) or non-displacement mode, automatic or chosen. Relative motion Hrm, Pm = 10 fz Hrm, Pp = 10 Hpm, Ps by Table 2.4.1 (interpolated to Pd above the waterline), impact Pdh (displacement) or Pdl (planing, av from θB, θD, Bc, BW, H1/3), forebody Pf interpolated along the length, weather deck Pwh / Pwl with fL and E, deckhouses C1 Pd, bulkheads 7,2 / 11,2 hb, cargo WCDP (1 + 0,5 ax). Design pressures by Pt 5 Ch 3 Table 3.3.1 / Ch 4 Table 4.3.1 with Hf, Sf, Gf, Cf and δf = 0,8 secondary, 0,5 primary and transverse frames.
  • SSC – scantlings. t = 22,4 s γ β √(p ks/(fσ 235)) 10⁻³; Z = ΦZ p s le² ks/(fσ 235); I = ΦI fδ p s le³/E · 100; Aw = ΦA p s le ks/(100 fτ 235/√3), with fσ, fτ and fδ of Pt 6 Ch 7 and load model (a) for primary and (b) for secondary members (selectable); minimum thicknesses of Table 3.2.1 with ω and kms; stiffener proportions of Table 3.1.2; keel, stem, bottom girders and floors of Sections 3, 5 and 6; pillars of Section 10.
  • Steel. k = 235/σo ≥ 0,66; kL 0,92 / 0,78 / 0,74 / 0,72 / 0,68 for hull girder (Ships); ks = 235/σs ≥ 0,66 and ηHTS (SSC).
  • Rounding. Required thickness + owner's margin to the nearest 0,5 mm (SSC Pt 6 Ch 3 1.6.1).

Not covered by this sheet

  • Ships: fore and aft end regions (Pt 3 Ch 5 / Ch 6, bottom slamming forward, panting), machinery space and engine seatings, hatch covers and coamings, buckling (Pt 3 Ch 4 Sec 7), shear strength, ship type chapters for bulk carriers, container ships, tankers (CSR) and dredgers beyond the Ch 1 basis.
  • SSC: multi-hull and cross-deck structure (Pt 6 Ch 4), foil craft, aluminium and composite construction (Pt 7 / Pt 8), windows, special features (Ch 5), buckling control (Ch 7 Sec 4) and shear strength.
  • Rules for Offshore Units: not included – floating offshore installations follow a different design basis and can be added as a separate calculator.

Rule references

  • Lloyd's Register Rules and Regulations for the Classification of Ships, January 2016: Pt 3 Ch 2 (materials), Ch 3 (structural design, design loading), Ch 4 (longitudinal strength), Ch 8 (superstructures and deckhouses); Pt 4 Ch 1 to Ch 6.
  • Lloyd's Register Rules and Regulations for the Classification of Special Service Craft, July 2022: Pt 5 Ch 2 to Ch 5, Pt 6 Ch 2, Ch 3, Ch 6 and Ch 7.
  • Reference calculation: P3094 60 m yacht SSC scantling workbook by Ansal Faris.
  • Scantlings are subject to approval by Lloyd's Register. This sheet is a design aid.
