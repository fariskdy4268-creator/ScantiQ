4 · Basis & references

Basis of calculation

  • Scope. Prescriptive local scantlings of ABS steel vessels and barges. Two rule sets: the Marine Vessel Rules Part 3 Chapter 2 (2026), which since 2024 cover all self-propelled steel vessels of any length – the former Steel Vessel, Steel Vessels under 90 m and OSV rules – with their length-dependent and OSV-specific formulas; and the Steel Barge Rules Part 3 Chapter 2 with the Part 5 requirements for deck cargo barges, dry cargo barges and single / double skin tank barges.
  • Gross scantlings. ABS rule scantlings already include the corrosion margin. An owner's extra margin can be entered on the ship tab (all plating) or per plate. Required plate thickness is rounded to the nearest 0,5 mm before comparison, as in the reference workbook.
  • Length bands (MVR). L < 61 m: hull girder SM by the small vessel C1 table, no wave moments; 61–90 m: C1 = 0,044 L + 3,75; ≥ 90 m: 10,75 curve. Side shell: OSV formula, vessels under 90 m (s √h / 268 + 2,5) or vessels 90 m and over ((s/645)√((L − 15,2) ds/Ds) + 2,5, L ≤ 305 m; (s/828) form to 427 m). Bottom shell transverse / longitudinal for all lengths with ds/Ds ≥ 0,67 (OSV) or 0,0433 L/Ds. Frames, beams, webs and superstructures switch at 90 m as the rules do.
  • Heads. MVR: freeboard / bulkhead deck heads for framing and watertight bulkheads (0,8 h + 1,22 below 6,1 m), Table 2 deck heads interpolated in L, deep tank head = greatest of the overflow, Table 2 column e, load line and 2/3 bulkhead deck heads, relief valve and SG > 1,05 corrections. Barges: void spaces to the deck at side; ballast tanks to 2/3 of the overflow height, ≥ 0,915 m; cargo tanks h1 = ρ ht + ha with ha = max(1,22 / 0,02 L / 2,44 ; 9,95 pv) (5-2-1/3).
  • Higher-strength steel. Q = 0,78 (32), 0,72 (36), 0,68 (40). Bottom plating (t − 4,3) Q + 4,3; side plating (t − 4,3)(Q + 2√Q)/3 + 4,3; decks per 3-2-3/7 with the 0,92/√Q factor not less than 1; bulkhead plating with q = 235/Y; section moduli × Q. MVR deck beams and deck girders are taken without Q.
  • Superstructure and deckhouse bulkheads. h = a (b f − y) c (IACS UR S3, 3-2-11/3.5), t = 3 s √h, SM = 3,5 s ℓ² h, with the minimum heads and the 2026 minimum thicknesses for OSVs, towing vessels and other vessels.
  • Hull girder. MVR: SM = max(SMmin ; Mt / 17,5) with Mt from the still water moments entered and the rule wave moments, I ≥ L SM / 33,3. Barges: SMR = K SMb, K = 0,629 + Ms/(fp SMb) ≥ 1, I = 0,03 SMR L. Required SM is multiplied by Q of the deck and bottom material.
  • Section modulus offered. Built from the profile and attached plating (FB, HP bulb as equivalent angle, L, T or entered value). Effective plating: s for stiffeners, min(spacing ; 0,33 ℓ) for girders and webs, as in the reference workbook. Primary member web depth and thickness proportions are checked.

Not covered by this sheet

  • Hull girder section properties are entered, not calculated; shear strength (3-2-1/3.9), buckling (3-2-A4) and fatigue; SafeHull / Part 5C direct strength for tankers, bulk carriers and container carriers of 150 m and above (these vessels also need Part 5C).
  • Ice strengthening, special docking arrangements, sea chests, hatch covers, doors, bulwarks, rudders and appendages; aluminium superstructures (3-2-11/13).
  • Bottom shell reduction factor Rb, slot depth proportions, bracket and knee table checks, end connections and weld sizes.
  • Specific Part 5 barge notations (crane, pipe-lay, accommodation, hopper, liquefied gas) beyond the common items here.

Rule references

  • ABS Rules for Building and Classing Marine Vessels 2026, Part 3 Hull Construction and Equipment (July 2026): 3-1-1 definitions, 3-2-1 hull girder, 3-2-2 shell, 3-2-3 decks, 3-2-4 bottom structures, 3-2-5 frames, 3-2-6 web frames and stringers, 3-2-7 beams, 3-2-8 pillars and girders, 3-2-9 watertight bulkheads, 3-2-10 deep tanks, 3-2-11 superstructures and deckhouses, 3-2-12/13 stem and keel.
  • ABS Rules for Building and Classing Steel Barges 2026 (January 2026): Part 3 Ch.2 Sec.1–8 and Part 5 Ch.1–2 (deck cargo, dry cargo, single and double skin tank barges).
  • Reference calculation: P4062 GTO 50 scantling workbook (40 m double skin tank barge) by Ansal Faris.
  • Scantlings are subject to approval by ABS. This sheet is a design aid.
