4 · Basis & references

Basis of calculation

  • Scope. DNV-RU-SHIP Pt.3 Hull for monohull steel ships of any length: local scantlings of plating, ordinary stiffeners and primary supporting members by the prescriptive rules (Ch.6), with the special requirements of Ch.10. Ship particulars and symbols per Ch.1 Sec.4: L0 = max(L; 110), L1 = min(L; 250), L2 = min(L; 300).
  • Approach. Each member is defined by its position, geometry and the compartment on each side. The compartments decide the design load sets of Ch.6 Sec.2 Table 1 (SEA-1/2, WB-1 to WB-4, TK-1 to TK-3, INT-1, FD-1, UDL-1/2, WL-1/2) and of Ch.6 Sec.8 Table 1 for superstructures, and the corrosion addition of Ch.3 Sec.3. Every design load set is evaluated and the worst governs.
  • Dynamic load cases (L > 90 m). For each dynamic set the 22 equivalent design waves HSM-1/2, HSA-1/2, FSM-1/2, BSR-1/2-P/S, BSP-1/2-P/S, OST-1/2-P/S and OSA-1/2-P/S are evaluated with the load combination factors of Ch.4 Sec.2 Tables 4–6 (fβ = 0,8 for BSR and BSP). Motions and accelerations: Ch.4 Sec.3, fps = fr. Wave pressures PW: Ch.4 Sec.5 [1.3] with the amplitude, phase and girth coefficients of Tables 1–18; above the waterline PW = PW,WL − ρ g (z − TLC).
  • Envelope loads (L ≤ 90 m). PW = PENV = max(PENV-BS; PENV-HS) at TSC (Ch.4 Sec.5 Table 19), envelope accelerations ax-env, ay-env, az-env (Ch.4 Sec.3 [3.3]) and the design load scenarios of Ch.4 Sec.7 Table 2, where PW is not combined with internal pressures. Can be switched on the ship tab.
  • Loading conditions. Full load at TSC and ballast at TBAL with kr = 0,39 B (0,35 B tankers in ballast) and GM by ship type, not less than 0,05 B, unless entered. Tank testing and harbour sets at Ttest = min(TBAL; 0,25 TSC).
  • Internal pressures (Ch.4 Sec.6). Pls-1 = ρL g (zTOP − z) + PPV; Pls-2 (ballast water exchange); Pls-3 = ρL g (zTOP − z) + P0 (harbour); Pls-4 = ρL g (zTOP − z + hair) + Pdrop; Pls-ST = 10 (zST − z) with zST = max(zTOP + hair; zTOP + 2,4; zbd) for double bottom, side and peak tanks. Pld = ρL [aZ (z0 − z) + aX (x0 − x) + aY (y0 − y)] with the reference point at the tank top corner maximising Vj (box tank between the x, y, z limits entered). Outer shell sets combine Pin − (PS + PW). Flooding Pfs = ρ g hfs. Deck loads Pdl-s (1 + aZ/g); wheel loads Pwl-1, Pwl-2 (Ch.10 Sec.5).
  • Exposed decks and superstructures. Green sea PD = max(χ PD-min; PW,D) (Ch.4 Sec.5 [2.2], Table 31), interpolated port to starboard for the beam and oblique cases, χ from the height above the freeboard deck; superstructure side max(PW; PSI); end bulkheads and deckhouse sides PA = fn fc [fb fd − (z − TSC)] ≥ PA-min (Ch.4 Sec.5 [3.4]) with Ch.6 Sec.8 scantlings.
  • Hull girder stress. σhg = [(Msw + Mwv-LC)(z − zn)/Iy − Mwh-LC y/Iz] 10⁻³, tension positive, with Mwv-LC = fβ CWV Mwv-h (CWV ≥ 0) or fβ CWV |Mwv-s|, Mwh-LC = fβ CWH Mwh (Ch.4 Sec.4) and Msw distributed with fsw. Hull girder Z checked against ZR-gr (Ch.5 Sec.2) and σperm = 175/k amidships.
  • Scantlings. Plating t = 0,0158 αp b (|P| / (Ca ReH))^0,5 (Ch.6 Sec.4, Table 1); stiffeners Z = fu |P| s ℓbdg² / (fbdg Cs ReH) and tw = Cm fshr |P| s ℓshr / (dshr Ct τeH) (Ch.6 Sec.5, Tables 1–5); primary members Z = 1000 |P| S ℓbdg² / (fbdg Cs ReH) and Ashr = 10 fshr |P| S ℓshr / (Ct τeH) with the load models of Ch.6 Sec.6 Table 1 (gross offered section). Minimum thicknesses Ch.6 Sec.3. Curved bilge, stern frame shell and berthing impact plating where selected.
  • Net scantlings. tc = tc1 + tc2 + 0,5 (internal members 2 tc1 + 0,5), not more than 0,2 tgr-off; gross required = net + tc rounded to the nearest 0,5 mm. Stiffeners net of full tc; bulb flats as equivalent angles. Effective breadth min(200 ℓbdg; s) for stiffeners, Ch.3 Sec.7 formula for primary members.
  • Special requirements (Ch.10). Bow impact PFB = C (2,2 + Cf)(0,4 V sin β + 0,6 L^0,5)² (Sec.1); bottom slamming PSL = c1 c2 BB (0,56 − L/1250 − x/L)/TF (Sec.2); stern slamming PSS (Sec.3); wheel loads (Sec.5); stem, propeller post, sea chest, thruster tunnel, engine seating and berthing (Sec.6). Plates t = 0,0158 ka b (Ppl/(Cd ReH))^0,5, Cd = 1,5; stiffeners Pst = 0,5 P with plastic section modulus; primary members PPSM = 0,4 P.
  • Slenderness (Ch.8 Sec.2). Plate tp ≥ b/C, C = 100 (L ≥ 90 m) or 125 for shell and strength deck, 125 elsewhere, 175 for internal structure of ships with more than three decks and deckhouses; stiffener web tw ≥ hw/Cw (ReH/235)^0,5 with Cw = 75 angle and T, 45 bulb, 22 flat bar; flange tf ≥ bf-out/12 (ReH/235)^0,5; primary member web tw ≥ sw/100 (125 double skin) (ReH/235)^0,5; tripping bracket spacing Sb ≤ C bf between 3,0 m and Sb-max.
  • Prescriptive buckling (Ch.8 Sec.3). For every design load set and load case the hull girder stress σhg (compression +) acts with the lateral pressure; stress combinations (σhg; 0,7 τhg) and (0,7 σhg; τhg). Elementary plate panel, simply supported, ψ = 1; stiffeners by overall stiffened panel (γGEB) and ultimate capacity for stiffener and plate induced failure, with the closed-form method of DNV-CG-0128. ηall = 0,80 for AC-I and 1,00 for AC-II and AC-III. σhg and τhg can be entered on each member to override.

Not covered by this sheet

  • Direct strength (FE) analysis (Ch.7), fatigue (Ch.9), hull girder ultimate strength, shear strength and torsion; buckling of primary member web panels, openings, pillars and corrugations; ψ ≠ 1 and non-simply supported plate edges.
  • Dry bulk cargo, steel coil, container and wheeled cargo pressure distributions of Pt.5 (only Pdl-s and wheel loads are included); sloshing and impact in partly filled tanks; hatch covers, doors, bulwarks and appendages.
  • Grillage or beam analysis for stiffeners and PSM with non-standard end fixity; curved stiffeners; double skin PSM by FE.
  • Hull girder shear stresses are not calculated: enter τhg on members where shear buckling matters (side shell and longitudinal bulkheads near the quarter lengths).
  • Additional class notations (ice, Pt.5 / Pt.6 notations) and service restriction specific reductions other than fr.

Rule references

  • DNV-RU-SHIP Pt.3 Hull, editions July 2024 / July 2025: Ch.1 Sec.4 (symbols), Ch.3 Sec.3 (corrosion additions), Ch.3 Sec.6–7 (structural idealisation), Ch.4 Sec.2–7 (load cases, motions, hull girder loads, external and internal loads, design load scenarios), Ch.5 (hull girder strength), Ch.6 Sec.2–8 (local scantlings), Ch.8 Sec.2–3 (slenderness and buckling), Ch.10 Sec.1–6 (special requirements).
  • DNV-RU-SHIP Pt.5 Ch.1 and Pt.6 Ch.1 (ship type and notation requirements) to be applied in addition where relevant.
  • DNV-CG-0128 Buckling (closed-form capacity used for Ch.8 Sec.3).
