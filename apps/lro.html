4 · Basis & references

Basis of calculation

  • Rules. Lloyd's Register Rules and Regulations for the Classification of Offshore Units, 2016 – Pt 4 Steel Unit Structures: Ch 2 materials (k = 235/σo ≥ 0,66), Ch 3 structural idealisation (effective plating, spans), Ch 4 unit types, Ch 5 permissible stresses and Ch 6 local strength.
  • Unit types. Column-stabilised units (semi-submersibles, TLPs, sea bed-stabilised units), self-elevating units and buoys / deep draught caissons. Ship units and other surface type units are designed to the Rules for Ships (Pt 4 Ch 4 Sec 4 and Pt 10) – use the LR ship calculator for those.
  • Design heads (Table 6.2.1). E = (0,0914 + 0,003 L)/(D − T) − 0,15 within 0 … 0,147, T = To (or TT for self-elevating units); h1 = 1,28 + 2,04 E or 0,14 pa + 2,04 E; h2 1,28, h3 2,0, h5 1,2, h6 0,90 / 0,60 / 0,45, h7 0,64 m, or 0,1417 p for higher specified loads. Watertight head h4 to 0,91 m above the bulkhead deck or the worst damage waterline; tank head to the tank top or half way to the overflow top.
  • Shell boundaries (Sec 3). Column-stabilised: t = 0,004 s f √(ho k) + 2,5 ≥ 9,0, Z = 8,5 s k ho le² 10⁻³, webs 8,5 k ho S le², ho to min(1,4 To ; upper hull) ≥ 6,0; upper hull by the watertight head h4 ≥ 6,0 with 0,012 s1 √k ≥ 7,5. Self-elevating: bottom and side shell of Table 6.3.3 with tm = (6,5 + 0,033 L) √(k s1/sb), framing 11,0 / 8,0 s k hT le² 10⁻³ with hT to min(1,6 TT ; D) ≥ 0,01 L + 0,7. Buoys and caissons: Table 6.3.5 with ho to the wave crest or deck + 1,0. Shell is never less than a watertight or tank bulkhead in the same position.
  • Decks, bulkheads, double bottom (Sec 4, 7, 8). Deck plating of Table 6.4.1, deck stiffeners C s k h le² 10⁻³ (le ≥ 1,5 m), girders 4,75 k S Hg le² and 11,7 ρ k h4 S le² in tanks; bulkhead plating 0,004 s f √(h4 k) ≥ 5,5 or 0,004 s f √(ρ h4 k/1,025) + 2,5 ≥ 7,5, stiffeners s k h4 le²/(71 γ (ω1 + ω2 + 2)) or ρ s k h4 le²/(22 γ (ω1 + ω2 + 2)); self-elevating double bottom dDB = 28 B + 205 √TT ≥ 760 with girders, floors and inner bottom of 8.3.
  • Helicopter landing area (Sec 5). Plating t = α s/(1000 √k) + 1,5 with P1 = 2,5 φ1 φ2 φ3 f γ Pw and β = log10(P1 k²/s² · 10⁷); α is read from a digitised copy of Fig 5.6.1, so check values near the chart limits. Stiffening and girders for emergency landing, normal operation and at rest (Table 6.5.1) against the permissible stresses of Table 6.5.2, with the patch load at mid-span of a fixed-ended member.
  • Erections (Sec 9). h = α δ (β λ − γ) with γ from the maximum transit waterline; t = 0,003 s √(k h) (0,0036 on the lowest-tier front of self-elevating units); Z = 0,0035 h s ls² k; deck plating of Table 6.9.5. Erections not subject to wave forces use h = 1,25 + 0,005 L2.
  • Rounding. Required thickness + owner's margin to the nearest 0,5 mm.

Not covered by this sheet

  • Global strength, the direct calculation of primary structure, buckling (Ch 5 Sec 3, 4), fatigue (Ch 5 Sec 5) and the load combinations of Ch 3 Sec 4 – the permissible stresses are shown for use with your own analysis.
  • Jack-up legs, spudcans, jacking and fixation systems, preload, TLP tendons and foundations, turrets, mooring and towing attachments, sliding bearings and the crane pedestal.
  • Ship units (Pt 10) and other surface type units – use the Rules for Ships (LR ship calculator).
  • Aluminium helidecks and structure, wheel-loaded decks (Sec 6), bulwarks (Sec 10) and machinery seatings.

Rule references

  • Lloyd's Register Rules and Regulations for the Classification of Offshore Units, 2016: Pt 4 Ch 1 to Ch 6.
  • Scantlings are subject to approval by Lloyd's Register. This sheet is a design aid.
