Basis of calculation

  • Rule set by length. L < 65 m: Ch 8 Sec 1 loads with Ch 8 Sec 3 to 5 net scantlings, or the Ch 8 App 1 gross formulae as the permitted alternative. 65 m ≤ L < 90 m: Ch 8 with the buckling checks of Sec 3 [5] and Sec 4 [4]. L ≥ 90 m: Ch 5 loads in load cases a, b, c and d with Ch 7 Sec 1 to 3 net scantlings.
  • Co-ordinates. x from the aft end of L, y from the centreline, z from the baseline (Ch 1 Sec 2 [4]). g = 9,81 m/s², ρ = 1,025 t/m³, Ry = 235/k N/mm².
  • Net scantlings. Ch 7 and Ch 8 Sec 3 to 5 results are net. Gross plate thickness = net + tc from Ch 4 Sec 2 Tab 2 (sum of both sides), rounded to the nearest half millimetre (Ch 1 Sec 1 [3.1.1]). Offered sections are reduced by tc (twice the one-side value for a member inside a compartment) before modulus and shear area are calculated. A bulb flat is converted to the equivalent angle of Ch 4 Sec 3 [3.1.2].
  • Hull girder stress, L < 90 m. σX1 from Ch 8 Sec 3 Tab 3, Sec 4 Tab 2 and Sec 5 Tab 4. ZAB and ZAD are limited to 2 ZREQ as the plating table states; the stiffener and primary member tables print "not less than", which would cap the stress instead, so the plating wording is applied throughout (conservative reading).
  • L ≥ 90 m. All four load cases are evaluated and the worst governs. Still water bending moments are applied as entered at every x. Hull girder shear stress for side, inner side and longitudinal bulkhead plating uses the preliminary values of Ch 7 Sec 1 Tab 5. Inclined-ship liquid pressure assumes a rectangular tank between the inboard and outboard y entered.
  • Buckling. Plate panels are checked for uniform uni-axial compression (ψ = 1). Longitudinals are checked for column and web buckling.
  • Regions. Each member belongs to a region: central part (Ch 7 / Ch 8), fore part forward of the collision bulkhead (Ch 9 Sec 1), aft part aft of the after peak bulkhead (Ch 9 Sec 2), machinery space (Ch 9 Sec 3) or superstructures and deckhouses (Ch 9 Sec 4). The region decides the loads, partial safety factors and minimum thicknesses applied.
  • Fore and aft parts. Sea pressure, exposed deck pressure (19,6 n φ H^0,5 forward, 17,5 n φ aft) and tank pressures of Tab 2 to 5, without hull girder stress. γR = 1,20 plating, 1,40 ordinary stiffeners, 1,60 primary members; divisor 8 for bottom and side members, m = 12 or 8 for deck stiffeners. Side transverses use the λ coefficients of Ch 7 Sec 2 [3.4.5]; deck primary members follow Ch 7 Sec 3. Floors, girders, panting beams, platform transverses, stems and propeller posts are checked against their dimension tables. For L < 65 m, plating and ordinary stiffeners may use Ch 8 App 1 as the rule permits.
  • Bottom impact (Ch 9 Sec 1 [3]) is checked for bottom members between ξL and 0,05 L aft of the fore end, up to 2L mm above the base line, when the minimum forward draught TF is entered and is less than min(0,04 L; 8,6 m), in any region. Bow flare impact ([4]) is checked for side members forward of 0,9 L above the waterline when the flare angle α and entry angle β are entered.
  • Machinery space. Ch 7 / Ch 8 scantlings plus the minimum net thicknesses of Ch 9 Sec 3 Tab 1 (double bottom) and Tab 2 (single bottom), platforms ([5.2.1]), casings ([7.3.2]) and the machinery seating guidance of Tab 3.
  • Superstructures and deckhouses. Walls not contributing to longitudinal strength use p = 10 a c [b f − (z − T)] ≥ pmin with t = 0,95 s (k p)^0,5 − tc and w = 0,35 φ k s ℓ² p (1 − α tc) − β tc. Contributing sides use Ch 7 / Ch 8 with sea pressure; tank boundaries are checked with hull girder stress 0; wood-sheathed decks may be 10 % thinner.
  • Appendix 1. Formula values are reduced for the navigation notation as in App 1 Tab 1 (half the reduction for decks and bulkheads); tabulated minima are not reduced. C4 = 0,10 (App 1 Tab 7) and tMIN = (3,7 + 0,0015 L k^0,5) cT (Ch 8 Sec 5 [2.1.1]; Ch 7 Sec 3 has 0,015) are applied as printed.

Not covered by this sheet

  • Direct calculations required by the Society (primary members in the bow flare area, fore peak structures of unusual layout, L > 120 m three dimensional analysis).
  • Bulbous bow arrangement details, thruster tunnels (to be not thinner than the adjacent shell), rudder horn and rudder post (Ch 10), sternframe gudgeons and sterntubes, movable wheelhouse supports.
  • Sloshing and impact pressures in tanks (Ch 5 Sec 6 [2]), wheeled loads, pillars, struts of open floors, corrugated bulkheads.
  • Shear and bi-axial plate buckling, torsional buckling of stiffeners, ultimate strength, fatigue and direct calculations.
  • Additional requirements for the service notation in Part E.

Where this sheet differs from the reference Excel

  • Plate aspect ratio coefficient ca (Local Scantling O3): the workbook adds 0,69 s/ℓ and does not cap the result; the rule is ca = 1,21 (1 + 0,33 (s/ℓ)²)^0,5 − 0,69 s/ℓ ≤ 1. The workbook value gives about 46 % thicker plating.
  • Intact / flooding divisor (Local Scantling Z14): the "Inact" spelling makes every stiffener use the flooding divisor 16 cP; intact conditions use 12. The workbook modulus is about 35 % low (unconservative).
  • Wave height h1 (Data Base M63): the rule limits h1 to not greater than D − 0,9 T; the workbook uses MAX instead of MIN.
  • h1 at the aft end (Data Base M61): 435/√CB should read 4,35/√CB. Effective only when CB < 0,875.
  • Surge / pitch acceleration aX1 (Ship Acc C22): the square is applied to (z − T) only instead of to the whole term in brackets.
  • Roll amplitude AR (Ship Acc D14): aB·MAX(E, 1) should be aB·√E. Effective only when E > 1.
  • Deck coefficient φ (Data Base D55:D59): the Ch 5 deck table is used; Ch 8 Sec 1 Tab 7 gives 0,42 for the second tier and above (L < 90 m).
  • Wall pressure coefficient b (Data Base E105): the factor 1,5 for x/L > 0,45 is missing, so front walls forward of 0,45 L are under-loaded.
  • Bracket coefficient βb (Local Scantling S14): denominator 0,9χ + 0,2 should be 0,8χ + 0,2.
  • Additional checks here not in the workbook: shear area, minimum net thicknesses, web and flange proportions, buckling, and Ry = 235/k in place of ReH.

Rule references

  • Tasneef Rules for the Classification of Ships 2016, Part B, Vol. 1 – Ch 1 (definitions, rounding), Ch 4 Sec 2 (corrosion additions), Ch 4 Sec 3 (strength checks, attached plating), Ch 5 Sec 1 to 6 (motions, hull girder loads, sea and internal pressures).
  • Part B, Vol. 2 – Ch 6 (hull girder strength), Ch 7 Sec 1 to 3 (plating, ordinary stiffeners, primary supporting members, L ≥ 90 m), Ch 8 Sec 1 to 5 and App 1 (ships less than 90 m and less than 65 m).
  • Part B, Vol. 3 – Ch 9 Sec 1 (fore part), Sec 2 (aft part), Sec 3 (machinery space), Sec 4 (superstructures and deckhouses).
  • Scantlings are subject to approval by the Society. This sheet is a design aid.

Rule reference

Plating – Ch 7 Sec 1 [3] / Ch 8 Sec 3 [3]

t = 14,9 ca cr s [γR γm (γS2 pS + γW2 pW) / (λL Ry)]^0,5  longitudinally framed, contributing (λL = (1 − 0,95 (γm σX1/Ry)²)^0,5 − 0,225 γm σX1/Ry)

t = 17,2 ca cr s [ … / (λT Ry)]^0,5  transversely framed, λT = 1 − 0,89 γm σX1/Ry; not contributing: 14,9 with λ = 1

ca = 1,21 (1 + 0,33 (s/ℓ)²)^0,5 − 0,69 s/ℓ ≤ 1 ; cr = 1 − 0,5 s/r ≥ 0,75 ; γS2 = 1,0, γW2 = 1,2, γm = 1,02, γR = 1,2 (flooding 1,05, collision bulkhead 1,25)

Ordinary stiffeners – Ch 7 Sec 2 [3] / Ch 8 Sec 4 [3]

w = γR γm βb (γS2 pS + γW2 pW) / [12 (Ry − γR γm σX1)] (1 − s/2ℓ) s ℓ² 10³  cm³

ASh = 10 γR γm βs (γS2 pS + γW2 pW) / Ry (1 − s/2ℓ) s ℓ  cm²; flooding: divisor 16 cP with cP = 1,16, γW2 = 1,05

Primary supporting members – Ch 7 Sec 3 [3] / Ch 8 Sec 5 [3]

w = γR γm βb (γS2 pS + γW2 pW) / [m (Ry − γR γm σ)] s ℓ² 10³ ; m = 10 in general, 12 for bottom and side girders; attached plating width = min(s, 0,2 ℓ); γR = 1,02 (1,15 for girders)

Sea pressure – Ch 8 Sec 1 Tab 6 / Ch 5 Sec 5

Below waterline pS = ρ g (T − z), pW = ρ g h1 e^(−2π(T − z)/L); above pW = ρ g (T + h1 − z) ≥ 0,15 L ; h1 = 0,42 n C (CB + 0,7) ≤ D − 0,9 T amidships

Exposed decks: pW = 17,5 n φ within 0,5 L of the aft end, rising to 19,6 n φ H^0,5 at 0,75 L and forward

Internal pressures – Ch 8 Sec 1 Tab 8 to 16 / Ch 5 Sec 6

Liquid pS = ρL g (zL − z) ≥ ρL g (zTOP − z) + 100 pPV ; pW = ρL (aX1 ℓC/2 + aZ1 (zTOP − z)) ; flooding pS = ρ g (zF − z), pW = 0,6 ρ aZ1 (zF − z), each ≥ 0,4 g d0

Superstructures and deckhouses – Ch 9 Sec 4

p = 10 a c [b f − (z − T)] ≥ pmin ; plating t = 0,95 s (k p)^0,5 − tc ≥ (5 + 0,01 L) k^0,5 − tc (lowest tier) ; stiffeners w = 0,35 φ k s ℓ² p (1 − α tc) − β tc

Fore part – Ch 9 Sec 1

γR 1,20 / 1,40 / 1,60 (plate / stiffener / PSM), γW2 1,20. Plating t = 14,9 ca cr s [γR γm (γS2 pS + γW2 pW)/Ry]^0,5 ≥ cF (0,038 L + 7)(s k)^0,5 − cE ; cE = 1 (L ≤ 65), 3 − L/32,5 (65 < L < 90), 0 (L ≥ 90); cF = 0,9 forecastle sides

Stiffeners w = γR γm βb (γS2 pS + γW2 pW)/(8 Ry)(1 − s/2ℓ) s ℓ² 10³ (decks: m Ry, m = 12 / 8); web ≥ lesser of 1,5 L2^1/3 k^1/6 and tp. Floors hM = 0,085 D + 0,15, AP = 3,15 D (long.) / 1,67 D (trans.), tP = 0,4 D + 5 ≤ 14; side girders bA = 2,5 (180 + L), tA = (6 + 0,018 L) k^0,5

Panting beams AB = 0,5 L − 18, JB = 0,34 (0,5 L − 18) bB²; centreline bulkhead t = 6,5 + 0,013 L1, stiffeners w = 3,5 s ℓ² k (zTOP − zM); plate stem ts = 1,37 (0,95 + L3^0,5) k^0,5; bar stem tB = (0,4 L + 13) k^0,5

Bottom impact pBI = 25 n [0,004 − (TF/L)²] L1 L / TF ; t = 13,9 ca cr s [γR γm γW2 pBI/Ry]^0,5 (γR 1,30, γW2 1,10) ; w = γR γm βb γW2 pBI /(16 cP Ry)(1 − s/2ℓ) s ℓ² 10³ (γR 1,15)

Bow flare pFI = n CS CL CZ (0,22 + 0,15 tan α)(0,4 V sin β + 0,6 L^0,5)² ; t = 11 ca cr s [γR γm γW2 pFI/Ry]^0,5 ; w = γR γm βb γW2 pFI /(18 cP Ry)(1 − s/2ℓ) s ℓ² 10³

Aft part – Ch 9 Sec 2

Same partial safety factors and formulae as the fore part; exposed deck pW = 17,5 n φ; cE = 3 − L/30 for 65 < L < 90, cF = 0,8 poop sides. Floors tM = 6,5 + 0,023 L1 k^0,5; transom floors 9 + 0,023 L1 k^0,5; shell at sternframe t = 0,045 L k^0,5 + 8,5; platforms 1,3 + 0,004 L k^0,5 + 4,5 s (L < 120). Propeller posts: Tab 8 (single screw) / Tab 9 (twin screw), gross.

Machinery space – Ch 9 Sec 3

Double bottom (Tab 1): inner bottom [0,75 L^0,5 + 1,35 + 4,5 (s − 0,23 L^0,25)] k^0,5; margin plate L^0,5 k^0,25 + 1 (0,9 outside 0,4 L); centre girder 1,8 L^1/3 k^1/6 + 4 (1,55 … + 3,5); floors and side girders 1,7 L^1/3 k^1/6 + 1. Single bottom (Tab 2): centre girder 7 + 0,05 L2 k^0,5 (6 + …), floors 6,5 + 0,05 L2 k^0,5 (5 + …), floor height B/14,5. Platforms 0,018 L2 k^0,5 + 4,5. Casings 5,5 / 4 mm.

Seatings (Tab 3), X = P/(nr LE): bedplate area 40 + 70 X; bedplate t (240 + 175 X)^0,5 (+5 for one girder); total girder web (320 + 215 X)^0,5 or (95 + 65 X)^0,5; floor web (55 + 40 X)^0,5

Corrosion additions – Ch 4 Sec 2 Tab 2 (one side, mm)

Sea and air 0,5 · ballast tank 1,0 (upper zone 1,25) · cargo or fuel oil 0,5 to 1,0 · dry bulk hold 1,0 to 1,75 · hopper well 2,0 · accommodation 0 · other 0,5

Buckling – Ch 7 Sec 1 [5] / Ch 8 Sec 3 [5]

σE = π² E / (12 (1 − ν²)) (t/b)² K1 ε ; σc = σE for σE ≤ ReH/2, otherwise ReH (1 − ReH/4σE) ; check σc / (γR γm) ≥ σb, γR = 1,1
