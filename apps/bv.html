4 · Basis & references

Basis of calculation

  • Scope. NR467 Part B applies to ships outside the scope of NR600: cargo ships of L ≥ 65 m and non-cargo ships of L ≥ 90 m (Ch 1 Sec 1). Smaller ships are checked to NR600 automatically; the rule set can also be forced on the ship tab. NR467 hull form limits L < 500 m, CB > 0,6, L/B > 5, B/D < 2,5 (Ch 1 Sec 2).
  • NR600 (ships below the NR467 range). With Rules set to Automatic, cargo ships under 65 m and non-cargo ships under 90 m are checked to NR600 (March 2026): relative motion h1 and accelerations per longitudinal area (Ch 3 Sec 3–4), sea pressures on bottom, side and exposed decks, side shell impact, flat bottom forward impact, planing hull slamming, liquid, testing, flooding and deck loads, each acting alone (Ch 3 Sec 1 [3.1.1]). Gross scantlings with the permissible stresses of Ch 2 Sec 3 Tab 2–4: plating Ch 4 Sec 3, stiffeners Ch 4 Sec 4, primary members as isolated beams Ch 4 Sec 5, superstructures Ch 5 Sec 1, stems and machinery space Ch 5 Sec 2, service notation minima for barges, passenger ships, supply vessels and tugs (Ch 6 Sec 1). Hull girder σA = MV/Z ≤ 0,60 R with MW and MSW of Ch 3 Sec 2. NR600 items that refer to NR467 (stern frame) and NR467 seating / panting beam values are shown for guidance.
  • Approach (as BV MARS). Each member is defined by its position, geometry and the compartment on each side. The compartments decide the design load sets of Ch 7 Sec 2 Tab 1 (SEA, WB, TK, LC, DL, FD) and the corrosion additions of Ch 4 Sec 3. Every set is evaluated and the worst governs.
  • Dynamic load cases. For each static + dynamic set the 24 equivalent design wave load cases HVM1/2, FVM1/2, BR1/2-P/S, BP1/2-P/S, OHM1/2-P/S, OHS1/2-P/S and OVA1/2-P/S are evaluated with the load combination factors of Ch 5 Sec 2 Tab 8 to 13. Motions and accelerations: Ch 5 Sec 3. Hydrodynamic pressure PEDW with the coefficients of Ch 5 Sec 5 Tab 9 to 20 (weather and lee side), PSR roll term for BR, linear decay above the waterline.
  • Loading conditions. Full load at TSC (kr = 0,35 B, GM = 0,07 B or 0,12 B), normal ballast at TBAL (kr = 0,45 B, GM = 0,18 B or 0,33 B) and partial load at 0,6 TSC, unless entered. Harbour and tank test sets use 0,25 TSC (0,7 TSC for ro-ro and passenger ships).
  • Internal pressures. Pls = ρL g (zTOP − z) + Ppv; Pld = ρL [aX (x0 − x) + aY (y0 − y) + aZ (z0 − z)] with the reference point at the tank top corner maximising Vj and accelerations at the tank centre (box tank between the x, y, z limits entered). Tank testing PST from Ch 5 Sec 6 Tab 16. Outer shell sets combine Pin − Pex for WB and TK tanks. Flooding Pfs = ρ g (zFL − z).
  • Hull girder stress. σL = [(Msw + Mwv-LC)(z − zn)/Iy − Mwh-LC y/Iz] 10⁻³ (Ch 6 Sec 1), Msw hogging or sagging with the sign of CWV, both when CWV = 0. Still water moments are distributed with fsw (Ch 5 Sec 4 Fig 1). Harbour sets use Msw-p.
  • Scantlings. Plating Ch 7 Sec 4 [1.1.1], stiffeners Ch 7 Sec 5 [1.1], primary members Ch 7 Sec 6 [2.1] with the acceptance criteria AC-1, AC-2, AC-3 of the design load set (Kcorr 1,2 / 1,1 for tank testing). Impact loads (AC-4): bottom slamming, stern slamming, bow flare and berthing per Ch 5 Sec 5 [4] with Ch 7 Sec 4 [4], Sec 5 [1.3], Sec 6 [2.3].
  • Net scantlings. Gross = net + tc rounded to the nearest 0,5 mm; tc = tc1 + tc2 + 0,5 mm (≤ 0,2 tgross when tgross ≤ 10 mm). Stiffener section properties net of full tc; primary member properties net of 0,5 tc (n50). Bulb flats as equivalent angles. Effective breadth min(0,2 ℓbdg, s) for stiffeners and the Ch 4 Sec 6 formula for primary members.
  • Slenderness (NR467 Ch 9 Sec 1 [2] → NR615 Sec 2). Net plate tp ≥ b/C (ReH/235)^0,5 with C = 100 for the hull envelope and 125 elsewhere; stiffener web tw ≥ hw/Cw (ReH/235)^0,5 with Cw = 75 angle and T, 45 bulb, 22 flat bar; flange tf ≥ bf-out/12 (ReH/235)^0,5 and bf ≥ 0,2 hw; primary member web tw ≥ sw/100 (ReH/235)^0,5 with sw the web stiffener spacing, flange as above, and tripping bracket spacing Sb ≤ max(bf C (Af/(Af + Aw/3) · 235/ReH)^0,5 ; 3,0 or 4,0 m). Net thicknesses with the full tc (Ch 4 Sec 2 Tab 1). Members of superstructures not contributing to longitudinal strength are excluded.
  • Prescriptive buckling (NR467 Ch 9 Sec 1 [3] → NR615 Sec 3 and Sec 5). For every design load set and load case, the hull girder stress σhg = −σL (compression +) is applied with the lateral pressure: σx = σhg, σy = 0 for longitudinal stiffening, σx = 0, σy = σhg for transverse stiffening, τ = τhg as entered. Accidental flooding sets carry no hull girder stress. Elementary plate panel SP-A with ψ = 1, simply supported (Tab 4 cases 1, 2, 15, Flong = 1), interaction formulae of Sec 5 [2.2.1]. Stiffeners: overall stiffened panel γGEB (Sec 5 [2.1], LB2 = 6 s) and ultimate capacity (γc σa + σb + σw) S/ReH = 1 for stiffener induced and plate induced failure, with M0 from w0 = ℓ/1000, M1 from lateral pressure (ℓ²/24 continuous, ℓ²/14,2 or ℓ²/8 sniped), M2 for sniped ends and torsional stress σw (Sec 5 [2.3]). S = 1,0, divided by Kcorr = 1,1 for tank testing. ηall = 0,80 for AC-1 and 1,00 for AC-2 and AC-3 (Ch 9 Sec 1 Tab 1). σhg or τhg can be entered on each member to override.
  • NR600 buckling (Ch 4 App 1). Global loads: plating of hull girder members between 0,3 and 0,7 LWL checked with the interaction formulae of [2.4.2] (ψ = 1, simply supported), σ = (MSW + MW)(z − zn)/Iy with Iy and zn entered on the ship tab, SFbuck 1,35; longitudinal stiffeners checked as columns σc ≥ SF σ with σE = min(σE1 column, σE2 torsional, σE3 web) and SFbuck 1,45 secondary, 1,25 primary (Ch 2 Sec 3 Tab 1). Local loads on primary members (App 1 [3.3], Tab 3): attached plating, web and flange with M = Z σlocam 10⁻³ and T = Ash τlocam 10⁻¹, SFbuck 1,45 (Ch 2 Sec 3 Tab 4). Gross scantlings.
  • Regional requirements. Fore part minimum thicknesses, floors, stringers, panting beams, platform transverses and stems (Ch 11 Sec 1); machinery space double / single bottom minimum thicknesses and engine seatings (Ch 11 Sec 2); aft part minimum thicknesses, stern frame shell, transom floors and propeller posts (Ch 11 Sec 3); superstructure fronts, aft ends and deckhouse sides with Psd (Ch 11 Sec 5).

Not covered by this sheet

  • Buckling by direct strength analysis (NR615 Sec 4), primary member web panels in way of openings, pillars and corrugations; non-uniform edge stress (ψ ≠ 1) and non-simply supported plate edges; fatigue (Ch 10), hull girder ultimate strength and direct calculations (cargo hold FE models for L ≥ 150 m, Ch 8).
  • Dry bulk cargo pressures, steel coils, wheeled cargo, containers, sloshing and impact in partly filled tanks, corrugated bulkheads, hatch covers, doors.
  • Beam or grillage analysis for stiffeners with reduced end fixity or variable load (Ch 7 Sec 5 [1.7]); curvature correction KR,s of curved stiffeners (taken as 1).
  • Hull girder shear stresses are not calculated (they need the shear flow distribution of Ch 6 App 1): enter τhg on members where shear buckling matters, e.g. side shell and longitudinal bulkheads near the quarter lengths; and warping stresses for ships with large deck openings.
  • Additional requirements of Part D / Part E for the service notation (NR467) and of NR600 Ch 6 other than the minima listed above; multihulls, aluminium and composite hulls (NR561 / NR546); wheeled loads; pillars; NR600 combined global + local check (App 2).

Rule references

  • Bureau Veritas NR600 Hull Structure and Arrangement for the Classification of Cargo Ships less than 65 m and Non-Cargo Ships less than 90 m, March 2026: Ch 1–5, Ch 6 Sec 1.
  • Bureau Veritas NR467 Rules for the Classification of Steel Ships, Part B, July 2026: Ch 1 (application, symbols), Ch 4 Sec 3 (corrosion additions), Ch 4 Sec 6 (spans, effective breadth, load points), Ch 5 Sec 2 to 7 (load cases, motions, hull girder loads, external and internal pressures, design load scenarios), Ch 6 Sec 1 (hull girder stresses), Ch 7 Sec 2 to 6 (design load sets, minimum thickness, plating, stiffeners, primary members), Ch 11 Sec 1, 2, 3 and 5 (fore part, machinery space, aft part, superstructures).
  • Bureau Veritas NR615 Buckling Assessment of Plated Structures, July 2023 (applied through NR467 Pt B Ch 9 Sec 1 and NR600 Ch 4 App 1).
  • Rule-based approach modelled on Bureau Veritas MARS2000 (prescriptive 2D assessment of plates and ordinary stiffeners per compartment and design load set).
  • Scantlings are subject to approval by the Society. This sheet is a design aid.

NR600 – ships below 65 m (cargo) / 90 m (non-cargo): gross scantlings

Relative motion h1 (Ch 3 Sec 3 Tab 1), cargo: h1m = 0,36 n CW (CB + 0,7) ≤ min(T; D − 0,9 TB); non-cargo: h1m = (0,38 CW + 0,3) n ≤ T; CW = 0,625 (118 − 0,36 LW) LW 10⁻³, LW = 0,5 (LWL + LHULL). Four areas: 0–0,25 / 0,25–0,70 / 0,70–0,85 / 0,85–1,0 LWL.

Sea pressure: bottom ρ g (T + h1 − z0); side max[ρ g (T + h1 − z); ρ g (T + 0,4 B1 sin AR − z); pdmin] ≤ bottom; exposed deck pd = (p0 − 10 zd) φ1 φ2 φ3 ≥ pdmin. Side shell impact Ci n1 (55/40/30, fore 70/55/30). Flat bottom impact pBI = 62 C1 LWL^0,6.
